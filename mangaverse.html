Reading Direction

Right-to-Left (RTL) — Japanese Traditional

Most manga published in Japan is read right-to-left. Panels flow from right to left, top to bottom. The MangaVerse reader has an RTL toggle (←RL) so you can switch at any time.

Left-to-Right (LTR) — Western / Manhwa

Western comics and Korean manhwa typically read left-to-right. Some translated manga editions are also flipped to LTR for Western audiences.
